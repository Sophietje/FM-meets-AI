Introduces the concept of Intersymbolic AI, which is the combination of symbolic AI with subsymbolic AI. For example:
- Graph neural networks combined with symbolic proof to automate higher-order logic provers
- Combining neural network verification with linearization and hybrid systems theorem proving for safety verification of Neural Network Control Systems

Some examples of symbolic AI that are given include logic, games and planning.
Some examples of subsymbolic AI that are given include (un)supervised machine and reinforcement learning.

The paper calls for careful examination of combinations of symbolic and subsymbolic AI that are useful.
