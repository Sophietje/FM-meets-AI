Hello, I'm Sophie. A researcher with a background in software verification who is now working in the field of AI and semantics. I am trying to find out whether/how I can use my experience in verification/formal methods in my current job. I set up this digital garden as a way to explore this topic.

My intention is to get an idea of what has been tried and what questions are still open. This can be related to formal methods for AI, AI for formal methods, ontologies for specifications, all kinds of things really. Some keywords that I'll be looking for include:
- Formal methods
- Verification
- Provers
- AI
- Neural networks
- Robustness
- Specification generation
- LLMs
- Agent(s) / agentic frameworks
- Bias

If you have any relevant work to share, do let me know via an [issue](https://github.com/Sophietje/FM-meets-AI/issues) or email :)
